One prop design sheet (landscape 3:2): the reading machine's big eraser, an object of the town, in three views of ONE AND THE SAME object. Left two thirds of the canvas, stacked: (1) the whole eraser lying horizontally, its front face; (2) below it, the whole eraser seen from its narrow side (its thickness), same scale. Right third, full height: (3) the same eraser simply turned 90 degrees to stand vertically, enlarged about 1.5 times, so its two rounded rubber ends are OUTSIDE the canvas (cut off by the top and bottom canvas edges) while the whole paper sleeve and a little white rubber above and below it are visible.
Design: a long narrow block eraser, 16 cm long, 3 cm wide, 1.5 cm thick. White rubber; both short ends worn into soft rounded shapes. A paper sleeve wraps the middle two thirds (its joint is on the back face, so no seam or extra line is visible on the front or the side; the sleeve shows only its two edges and a thin teal border). Print on the sleeve's front face, single teal ink: the dot-matrix text 「読取機用」 written horizontally along the length, centred (each glyph made of separate round dots, like a receipt printer), and along the sleeve's lower edge a row of EXACTLY SIX small solid teal squares, evenly spaced, like answer-sheet timing marks. On the narrow side (2) the sleeve is a plain band with its border, no print.
View (3) is view (1) rotated 90 degrees clockwise, nothing re-laid-out: the text 「読取機用」 LIES ON ITS SIDE (read from top to bottom, each character rotated so its top points to the RIGHT), and the six squares form a column along the sleeve's LEFT edge. The same text size relative to the sleeve, the same six squares, the same border and the same proportions as view (1).

Drawing style: a printed illustration in ONE ink colour only, teal (#218EA3), on paper white (#F5F3ED), the same hand as the second attached image: clean confident teal line art (outer contour a little heavier, inner lines thin and few), shadows and darker materials as clean, regular, round teal screentone dots (halftone, one dot size for light tone, a denser one for dark tone), solid teal or very dense teal dots for black hair and very dark clothes. White areas are paper white. No other colours: no grey, no black, no brown, no gradients, no airbrush, no glow, no blur, no paper texture, no pencil sketchiness. It must look like a crisp printed page, as drawn by a professional anime character designer.
The rubber is paper white with teal screentone shading on its rounded ends; the sleeve is paper white printed teal. It must read as a real, solid object with weight, not a flat sticker.
Fully TRANSPARENT background (PNG alpha) everywhere outside the subject: no floor, no ground line, no cast shadow, no frame, no paper colour behind it. Every part of the subject is fully opaque, including its white and paper-white parts.
No captions or labels other than the printed 「読取機用」 on the sleeve. No watermark.
Absolutely no red, pink or orange anywhere.
