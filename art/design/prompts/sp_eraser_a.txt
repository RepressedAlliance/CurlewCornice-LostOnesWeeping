One prop design sheet (landscape 3:2): the reading machine's big eraser, an object of the town, in three views at the same scale: (1) the WHOLE eraser lying horizontally, front face; (2) the whole eraser seen from its narrow side (showing its thickness); (3) the eraser standing VERTICALLY, seen close and large, with only its middle part in the picture (its top and bottom ends cut off by the canvas edge), as it appears sweeping across a page.
Design: a long narrow block eraser, 16 cm long, 3 cm wide, 1.5 cm thick. White rubber; both short ends are exposed and worn into soft rounded shapes (the working edge a little more rounded than the other). A paper sleeve wraps the middle two thirds, printed in the single teal ink: down its length a line of large dot-matrix katakana-kanji text 「読取機用」 (each glyph made of separate round dots, like a receipt printer), and beside it a single column of separate small solid teal squares evenly spaced like the timing marks on an answer sheet; a thin teal border round the sleeve. On view (3) the text reads vertically, top to bottom.
Drawing style: a printed illustration in ONE ink colour only, teal (#218EA3), on paper white (#F5F3ED), the same hand as the first attached image: clean confident teal line art (outer contour a little heavier, inner lines thin and few), shadows and darker materials as clean, regular, round teal screentone dots (halftone, one dot size for light tone, a denser one for dark tone), solid teal or very dense teal dots for black hair and very dark clothes. White areas are paper white. No other colours: no grey, no black, no brown, no gradients, no airbrush, no glow, no blur, no paper texture, no pencil sketchiness. It must look like a crisp printed page, as drawn by a professional anime character designer.
The rubber is paper white with teal screentone shading on its rounded ends; the sleeve is paper white printed teal. It must read as a real, solid object with weight, not a flat sticker.
Fully TRANSPARENT background (PNG alpha) everywhere outside the subject: no floor, no ground line, no cast shadow, no frame, no paper colour behind it. Every part of the subject is fully opaque, including its white and paper-white parts.
No captions or labels other than the printed 「読取機用」 on the sleeve. No watermark.
Absolutely no red, pink or orange anywhere.
